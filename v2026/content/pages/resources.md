## Community

- **Questions & bugs** — open an issue in the respective repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/AngleSharp).
- **Contributing** — every repository contains a `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Small improvements to the docs are just as welcome as new features.
- **Sponsoring** — the project is maintained in spare time. If AngleSharp helps you or your company, consider [sponsoring the development](https://github.com/sponsors/FlorianRappl).
